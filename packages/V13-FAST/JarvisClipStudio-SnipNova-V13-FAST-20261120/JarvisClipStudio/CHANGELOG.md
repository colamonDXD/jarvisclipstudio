# Changelog

What changed in SnipNova for people using the product. Newest dates are first.

Add a line here when a pull request changes something a user can see. Use **New**, **Improved**, **Fixed**, or **Under the hood**, describe the change in plain language, and link the pull request. Skip internal-only work, or fold it into one **Under the hood** line. Do not include customer names, email addresses, user IDs, or anything else private.

## 2026-11-20
- **Fixed:** Swear words in captions, titles and clip questions are now hidden completely as *** — before, the first letter stayed visible (like F***), which was not the rule. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Streamer clips can now sequence their layout around the big moment instead of holding one layout for the whole clip: the split view gives way to full-screen gameplay for the three seconds before the peak (the cause), then full-screen facecam for four seconds from the peak (the reaction), then back to the split — the shape pro streamer edits use. The peak is the one the picker recorded for the clip, or the clip's own loudest second when none was recorded. Captions stay on the seam band and the credit line stays in place across every switch, and hand-drawn layout boxes always win over sequencing. Off by default — set `VIEW_SEQUENCING=on` to try it. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Clips can now end the way viral clips end, two ways, both off by default. With `PAYOFF_TIGHT=on`, a clip stops right after its payoff lands instead of trailing off: the dead air after the moment is trimmed away, the payoff's last spoken sentence still finishes whole, and a clip is never tightened under the minimum length. With `LOOP_ENDINGS=on`, when two endings are otherwise equal the picker prefers the one whose last spoken words match the clip's opening words (and whose last frame looks like the first frame), so the clip loops cleanly when a viewer replays it. The loop preference can only break a near-tie — it never overrides a clearly better ending. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Finished clips can now carry a comment-bait question — one narrow question about the clip's own moment ("Did he overreact or was that clutch?"), written by the AI ladder from the clip's words with a fixed per-topic question as backup. It is printed in the clip's post copy as the pinned comment to post first, shown in the post payload when you share the clip, and burned as a small end card over the clip's final two seconds (never on Plain — just cuts clips). No emoji is ever added. Off by default — set `COMMENT_BAIT=on` to try it. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Finished clips can now be checked for dead stretches — spans where the picture does not change for more than a few seconds, which is where viewers swipe away. With `BEAT_DENSITY=on`, every finished clip is measured: its longest static stretch and a beat score are recorded with the render and shown in the clip's quality report, and a clip whose longest stretch runs over 3.5 seconds gains one gentle punch-in in the middle of the dead stretch. Plain — just cuts clips are measured but never altered. Off by default. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Two optional caption-word passes can now run after word timings are aligned and before captions are burned, on every transcript source. The first asks the AI to tidy obvious speech-recognition word errors ("eleven" becomes "11", misheard names get their real spelling) under a strict contract: one corrected word out per word in, timings never touched, and the original words are kept whenever anything about the answer looks off. The second turns Hindi captions written in Devanagari script into the Roman Hinglish spelling viewers actually read, word by word, timings preserved. Both are off by default — set `CAPTION_WORD_FIX=on` and `TRANSLIT_HINGLISH=on` to try them. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** Moment picking on finished streams can now use an optional SenseVoice pass: it listens for laughter, applause, cheering and emotional speech (the SenseVoiceSmall model by Alibaba Group / FunAudioLLM) and feeds them to the picker as extra reaction signals, alongside a new "shout" signal that needs no model at all. Off by default — set `SENSEVOICE_FEATURES=on` to try it; the picks themselves do not change until the picker's weights for these signals are trained and approved. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** Clip openings can now be composed the way viral Shorts open (set `HOOK_FINISH_V2=on`): on top of the cold open, the frame-1 hook title and the punch-in cut, the frame starts punched in 1.3x on the face and settles back to normal over the first 1.7 seconds, and the sound plan guarantees one library sound hit inside the first second, ducked under the first words so it never covers them. Off by default; with it off, renders are unchanged. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))

## 2026-11-19
- **New:** Live clipping can now use boxes you draw yourself. When a face-on-top layout is picked, a "Draw boxes" step grabs one frame of the stream and lets you drag the facecam box (pink) and the gameplay box (cyan) right on the picture, with a live 9:16 preview of the stacked result and a face-on-top / face-below choice. Every clip of that session then uses exactly those crops instead of auto-detecting the facecam. Your boxes are saved per channel in the browser, so the same streamer's layout comes back next time. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** A clip that opens with a hook line moved to the front now keeps that opening when the 30-second minimum length stretches a short clip: the extra time is taken from the end of the clip, never by starting the hook line earlier and putting the video's original opening back in front of it. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** On the Live page, a finished stream can now be searched with "Find moments where…": type what you are looking for and the app searches the stream's full transcript plus the reaction signals, then clips the moments that match your words. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Live clipping's moment picking has been trained on real streams: the ranking weights were fitted against YouTube's "most replayed" data and now ship with the app, so the moments it picks first are the ones viewers actually rewatch. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Clips now open with a hook finish (Plain — just cuts is unchanged): a punch-in cut with a whoosh and a brief white flash in the first seconds, timed from a profile measured on 26 viral Shorts, and dark clips are lifted so they do not open murky. An optional funny-voice effect is available for comedy clips. Set `HOOK_FINISH=off` to turn the finish off. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Hook-finished clips carry a short quote title — a line from the clip itself, written in the third person — sitting small at the very top of the frame. The app adds no emoji under titles; add your own when you post. Set `HOOK_EMOJI=on` to bring the emoji row back. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** On the "Face on top" caption style, each word now turns yellow as it is spoken and stays yellow, so the line fills with colour as the sentence lands. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Emoji stickers can pop beside the facecam on laugh, fail and hype moments in streamer clips. Off by default: set `STICKERS=on` to try them. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Live picking on very long streams could break when the picker answered with many clips at once (up to 30); answers are now requested in batches and read tolerantly, and stream intros are skipped. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** The colour lift on clips pushed saturation too far (reds and skin tones overshot what viral clips measure); the correction is softer now. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** The renderer now accepts hand-drawn face and gameplay boxes for streamer layouts; the screen for drawing them on the Live page follows separately. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Clips made from a finished YouTube stream now use YouTube's own captions as the transcript when YouTube has them — the words and timings come from the platform instead of a speech model, so clips are ready faster and the captions match what was actually said. Streams without captions, and streams still live, use the speech models exactly as before. Set `TRANSCRIPT_YT_CAPTIONS=off` to always use the speech models. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Some streamer clips came out with no captions at all, or only a stray word or two, because the speech detector decided the streamer was never talking over the gameplay. When it finds no speech at all, the transcription now retries once without it — on the clip that exposed this, that recovered the streamer's actual words (32 of them) where before there were none. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Caption words now land on the voice. Word timings from every transcript source are snapped onto the clip's actual loudness before the captions are burned, so the spoken-word highlight no longer leads or trails the speaker, and every finished live clip is checked for speech that ended up with no caption over it (the verdict is recorded with the render). Set `CAPTION_WORD_ALIGN=off` to turn the alignment and the check off. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Hook titles on clips are no longer plain white, and no longer carry stray quote marks from the picking model. On the all-white seam caption styles, the title's key words and numbers now take the caption system's yellow accent, and auto-picked titles are cleaned before they can be used: quotes and emojis removed, first-person titles ("I lost it") dropped, and titles kept to eight words built from what the streamer actually said. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Campaign briefs can now carry hard rules — words a clip must mention, banned words, a shortest/longest clip length and a frame shape — and the rules are enforced when clips are chosen: a clip that breaks them is rejected and the reason is written into the task's brief report, instead of shipping and failing the campaign later. Turn it off with `CAMPAIGN_RULES_ENFORCE=off`. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Clips are no longer picked from ad breaks or "be right back" cards. One pass over the video maps the stretches where the picture barely moves, and a clip that mostly sits on such a slate is left out before rendering. A video that is still throughout (a podcast over cover art) keeps all its clips. Turn it off with `SLATE_EXCLUSION=off`. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** When a gaming video's title names the game (GTA VI and GTA V today), the clip picker gets rules for what counts as a moment in that game, and posts of the clip automatically carry the game's hashtags (like #GTA6) alongside your own. Turn it off with `GAME_RULES=off`. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** Words from a video — its transcript, title and description — are now cleaned before they are shown to the AI that picks clips, so speech in a video cannot pose as an instruction to the picker. Normal speech is unchanged. Turn it off with `PROMPT_SANITIZE=off`. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))

## 2026-11-17
- **New:** The bundled caption font library now includes ten short-form display fonts -- Luckiest Guy, Lilita One, Titan One, Bungee, Permanent Marker, Paytone One, Passion One, Rubik Mono One, Chewy and Fredoka -- all taken from the official Google Fonts repository under OFL-1.1 or Apache-2.0, with a manifest and checker that verify every bundled font. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** The extended transition library now has a public catalogue with family filters and cached 1.2-second preview clips, controlled by `TRANSITION_LIBRARY_API`; rendering is unchanged until the editor adopts it. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Clips can now be exported in a wide 16:9 YouTube frame (1920x1080) and a square 1:1 frame (1080x1080) as well as the three vertical formats. A vertical clip is fitted whole inside the wider frame -- never stretched or cropped -- with a softly blurred copy of the same picture filling the side or top bands, so burned-in captions stay inside the frame. The wide format follows YouTube's published 1080p upload settings (8 Mbps at 30 fps or below, 12 Mbps above, AAC 384 kbps); square uses the same 1080-class settings. The three existing vertical exports are unchanged. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Renders now use the graphics-card encoder again on newer FFmpeg builds. The startup check handed FFmpeg a filter-graph file option that FFmpeg 8-era builds have removed, so the check failed with "Unrecognized option 'filter_complex_script'" and every render quietly fell back to the processor. The check now passes its graph inline, exactly as production renders already do, and it validates the encoded file before deleting its temporary folder (it previously validated after the folder was gone, which failed on a real FFmpeg even when the encode had worked). ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Streamer clip picking no longer puts the loudest game moment first when the streamer didn't react to it. Several changes work together: **Mic on its own channel:** when the recording has the mic on one side and the game on the other, the app listens to the mic alone. **Screams count:** a scream counts as a reaction even when it isn't much louder than the streamer's normal talk. **Face measured itself:** the facecam's movement is measured on the face, not the room behind it. **Reaction and gameplay together:** a game event only ranks high when the streamer actually responds to it. On the reference test video the scream now ranks first and the loud explosion with no reaction is left out. Set `REACTION_FUSION=0`, `REACTION_MIC_CHANNEL=0` or `REACTION_FACE_ACTIVITY=roi` to switch the parts off. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Under the hood:** Streamer moments now also carry the reaction contract's fields. These are a 0–1 strength for each signal (facecam reaction, voice burst, emotional words, gameplay event; empty when not measured, never a fake zero), a fused 0–100 score, a one-sentence reason, and a layout recommendation. Existing fields are unchanged. Set `REACTION_CONTRACT_FIELDS=0` to leave them out. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **New:** Two "Face on top, game below" layouts for streamer clips, in the style of popular gaming pages. The streamer's facecam fills the top of the frame, cropped tight, and the gameplay fills the bottom. The captions sit on the seam a couple of punchy words at a time. A small "credit @creator" line can go at the bottom. **Red** has a red divider and white captions with a red outline; **Gold** has a gold divider and the spoken word in yellow. Pick one under "Gaming layout" on the create page when the niche is Gaming, and optionally type the creator's name for the credit. Clips without a facecam keep the normal layout. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Streamer clips now find the facecam even when it sits half-way down the side of the screen or the face is small, and even when the game shows no faces. The app looks for the camera window itself, checks there is a face in it, and then puts the streamer on top and the game below. It ignores video calls (several people in fixed windows) and stream overlays with a printed face. Set `FACECAM_RECT_DETECT=0` to switch this off. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** Video-call podcasts (each person in their own window) sometimes came out as the whole call shrunk into the middle of the vertical frame, with small faces. A still face in a top corner was being mistaken for a streamer's facecam. Now a facecam also needs a camera frame the app can actually measure; otherwise the clip gets the normal layout, which shows both people large, one above the other. Set `REFRAME_REACTOR_NEEDS_INSET=0` to restore the previous check. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Fixed:** On a computer with an NVIDIA graphics card but without the CUDA libraries installed (common outside the Docker image), transcription crashed or hung. It now switches to the processor once and remembers that for the rest of the run. `WHISPER_DEVICE=cpu` forces the processor. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Clip quality checks can now flag a clip that is far from the -14 LUFS loudness short-form platforms use, and a clip that opens or ends mid-sentence or cuts a word. Both are warnings only and run when the pipeline supplies the loudness target and transcript. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
- **Improved:** Talking videos now get roughly twice the stock B-roll, placed where it helps. Finance, podcast, education, vlog, travel and true-crime clips plan cutaways at 3–4.5 per minute instead of about one per clip, and each cutaway is chosen from what the speaker is actually saying ("interest rates" shows a central bank, not a random building). Words used as names ("Tim Cook", "Steve Jobs") and passing mentions no longer trigger footage. While the speaker says "look at this" the original picture stays on screen, and during a live reaction (laughing, "wow!") the stock shot appears as a framed picture-in-picture so the face stays visible. A stock search that fails because of a rejected key is now recorded as a provider error instead of "nothing found". Set `BROLL_CADENCE=legacy` to keep the previous behaviour. ([#TBD](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/supoclip/pulls))
